Privacy

This page is a plain-words summary written with the product. The owner's lawyer supplies the final text before launch.

The desktop app

Your chats, what she remembers about you and the pictures she makes are stored in a folder on your own computer. They are not sent to us.

What does leave your computer

  • Web searches she runs, and links you paste, are fetched from the sites concerned.
  • Her voice model is downloaded once, the first time it is used.
  • The app checks for updates.

Sharing with friends

If you make a link, your friends' chats are stored on your computer, and the app tells them so before they start.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

Your data

Export it to a file or delete it at any time from Settings. Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder removes everything.
