Fair questions.

Is it really free?
The desktop app is, for as long as you run it on your own computer. The browser version will be pay as you go, because then she is running on ours.
Who can read my chats?
On the desktop app, nobody but you. They are files on your computer and are not sent anywhere.
What computer do I need?
Windows 10 or 11. With a 6 GB graphics card or better she answers in a couple of seconds. On less she still runs, more slowly, and the app tells you what to expect before it downloads anything.
Is she for adults?
Yes, 18 and over only. Conversations can be flirtatious and explicit, and the app asks you to confirm your age before she says a word.
Can I take her with me?
Link your phone to your computer from Settings, or share a link. Your memory and chats come along.
What if I stop using it?
Uninstall it. Your data is in one folder; keep it or delete it. There is no account to close.
